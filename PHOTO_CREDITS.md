# Category photo credits

These real photographs illustrate categories. They do not depict or endorse CampusLoop providers, establish provider portfolio rights, or imply permission from the photographed people. No generated images are used.

Source file pages identify the creators and licenses below (checked 14 November 2026). Assets were resized and converted to WebP, with no subject alterations. CC BY-SA image derivatives remain under their stated CC BY-SA license; that license applies to the photo asset, not to the application code. CC0/public-domain entries retain their stated status.

Transport and the Keke vehicle card use the same `keke.webp`; every other category has its own photo. Roommates uses the source photograph “Roommate bonding,” showing a group together indoors in a shared room; it is illustrative, not a CampusLoop household.
